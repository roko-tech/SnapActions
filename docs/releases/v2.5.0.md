# SnapActions v2.5.0 — Translations in a native card

Translate now shows just the translation in a compact card beside your selection, instead of Google's full website squeezed into the popup.

- The card follows your light or dark theme, names the language pair and aligns right-to-left text such as Arabic correctly.
- **Copy** copies the translation. **Replace selection** pastes it over an editable selection, keeping spaces or line breaks around the selection.
- **⇄** swaps the languages and translates again. The swapped pair is saved for the next selection. It is hidden while the source is **Detect language**.
- **Open in Google Translate** turns the card into Google's full page when you want to edit the text or choose other languages.
- The translation still comes from Google's Translate website, loaded out of view. If SnapActions can't read the result within about six seconds of the page loading, for example after Google changes its page, the popup shows that page instead.

Translation still needs internet access, the Google Translate website and the [Microsoft Edge WebView2 Evergreen Runtime](https://developer.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/webview2/#download-section). The 500 UTF-8 byte limit and online-lookup consent are unchanged. See [how the card works](../translation-card-2026-09-27.md) for the design, measurements and limits.

## Validation

The release passed 639 .NET tests, including 17 new tests for result reading, whitespace-preserving Replace and the selection handoff. It also passed 12 browser tests, the compiled WPF checks (which now render the card's result, failure and page views) and three native-host subprocess cases.

An isolated harness showed the production card with synthetic text and Google's live page. It covered separate lines, sentences, paragraphs, single words, Detect language, swap, the page view, and a simulated Google page change that fell back to the page. Instrumented results appeared 3.4–4.3 seconds after the card opened, mostly spent loading Google's page.

On Windows 10 at 250% scaling, the installed candidate translated a Notepad selection from the toolbar and replaced it with the Arabic translation. The keyboard palette also opened the card with Replace available; swap produced English, and Replace after a swap kept Notepad focused. **Open in Google Translate** showed the page and Esc closed it. Saved settings were unchanged afterward. Copy was not clicked live, to leave the clipboard untouched.

The release includes `release-validation.json` for the committed package, hosted Windows CI and installed executable identity, and `live-validation.json` for the recorded desktop checks.

## Upgrade

Exit SnapActions and replace the complete application folder contents with the new ZIP. Keep the executable and accompanying files together. Existing saved preferences are retained. The browser companion is unchanged; register it again only if the executable moved.
